Understanding Motor Control:

PWM (Pulse Width Modulation): The microcontroller sends rapid on/off pulses to the motor driver. The ratio of "on" to "off" time controls the motor speed.

PID Correction: Based on sensor input, the algorithm calculates error and adjusts PWM values to:

• Move Straight: Both motors at equal PWM (50% = balanced speed)

• Turn Left: Left motor slower, right motor faster (unbalanced speeds)

• Turn Right: Right motor slower, left motor faster (unbalanced speeds)

• Sharp Curve: One motor much slower or stopped while other runs full speed

Closed-loop System: Sensor readings continuously feed back into the algorithm, allowing real-time PWM adjustments for smooth, responsive steering.
