How It Works:

5 IR Sensors: Arranged in a line across the robot's front to detect the position of the black line.

Detection Logic: When a sensor is over the black line, it reads as LOW (line detected). When over white surface, it reads as HIGH (no line).

Position Detection: By analyzing which sensors are active, the algorithm determines:

• Centered: Middle sensor active → Move straight

• Left Drift: Left sensors active → Steer right

• Right Drift: Right sensors active → Steer left

Real-time Response: This happens 100 times per second, allowing smooth, responsive steering!
